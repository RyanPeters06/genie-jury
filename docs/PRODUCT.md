# Product brief — Genie Jury

## The idea

Hackathon builders often ask a chatbot whether an idea is good and receive a flattering answer. Genie Jury is the useful opposite: a theatrical live pitch where four distinct AI jurors pressure-test real assumptions, check claims in a real browser, and leave the builder with the smallest credible next move.

The target user is a solo hackathon builder or early product founder deciding whether an idea is worth building before demo day. The primary success case is a 90-second demo that feels playful but produces one concrete scope cut, one evidence-backed question, one user-focused test, and four transparent scores.

## Experience

1. **Enter the sky.** The builder sees Ember, Gale, Tide, and Volt seated on clouds in a bright daytime sky and chooses live voice or typed fallback.
2. **Pitch like a real conversation.** The builder speaks naturally. The mic remains open, a juror may interrupt only for a real reason, and the builder can interrupt a juror back by speaking.
3. **Deliberate honestly.** The Bailiff assigns distinct angles. Gale researches risky claims through Browserbase's live browser while Ember, Tide, and Volt contribute independent findings through the shared ledger.
4. **Recover.** The Ally converts pressure into a narrow, buildable plan, evidence receipts, votes, and the four-part scorecard.

## Evidence standard

- **Verified:** an inspectable source supports the claim.
- **Contested:** credible sources disagree or the signal is inconclusive.
- **Unproven:** the claim needs a user test or further research.

The jury may be funny, but evidence language must never pretend a source exists when it does not.

## Visual direction

The supplied Genie Jury sprite sheet is canonical. Every juror sits forward on a cloud with crossed arms. The top row is listening; the bottom row is speaking. The stage uses soft daytime blue, clouds, sun bloom, and sparse speaker cues. The active juror rises and gains emphasis while the others recede.
